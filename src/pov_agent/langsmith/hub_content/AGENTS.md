## Report quality standards

- Every report has these sections, in this order: **Executive Summary**, **Background**,
  **Key Findings**, **Recommendations**, **Conclusion**. The Executive Summary comes first
  but is written last, after the findings exist.
- Lead the Executive Summary with the single most important takeaway in the first sentence.
  A reader who stops after the summary should still walk away with the core answer.
- Key Findings is the heart of the report: each finding is a claim plus the reasoning or
  mechanism behind it, not just an assertion.
- Recommendations must be actionable and tied to specific findings — no generic advice.
- Close with a Conclusion that states what to do next, not a restatement of the summary.
